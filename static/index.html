The Problem

Dividing work across Indonesia is harder than it looks

Your team operates across thousands of administrative areas. Splitting them into fair, balanced territories shouldn't require a geospatial engineering project.

Administrative complexity. Indonesia has provinces, cities, districts, and villages — each with changing boundaries. Manual division doesn't scale.

Unbalanced workloads. Random assignment creates territories with vastly different numbers of accounts, outlets, or delivery points.

No reusable system. Teams build one-off scripts or spreadsheets. When data changes, the process starts over.

Territory Builder

Define your operational areas using Indonesia's administrative boundaries.

Select areas. Combine them into territories. Use those territories in your operations.

Select Areas

Step 2

Step 3

Deploy

Why Territory Builder

Stop building territory logic from scratch

Territory Builder handles the administrative data complexity so your team can focus on operations.

No data maintenance

Official boundary data, versioned and current. No stale shapefiles or manual updates.

Balanced by default

Algorithm considers point density, workload targets, and area constraints simultaneously.

Indonesia-focused

Review before publish
